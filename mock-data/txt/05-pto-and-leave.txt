Paid Time Off and Leave
Document owner: People Operations
Last updated: 2026-01-08

Full-time employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed in their country of residence. Leave accrues monthly but may be taken in advance of accrual with manager approval.

Up to 10 unused days may be carried into the following year and must be used before 31 March. Days beyond that are forfeited. Nordvane does not pay out unused leave except where local law requires it on termination.

Requests are made in the people tool and approved by the direct manager. There is no minimum notice for one or two days. Absences longer than five working days should be requested at least three weeks ahead so on-call rotations can be adjusted. Two people from the same on-call rotation may not be on leave simultaneously without the rotation being rebalanced first.

Sick leave is not counted against annual leave and is not capped. Employees are asked to notify their manager and, if they are on the current rotation, the on-call channel. A doctor's note is requested only where local law requires one, typically after the fifth consecutive day.

Parental leave is six months at full pay for the primary carer and six weeks at full pay for the secondary carer, available to all employees regardless of gender and regardless of whether the child arrived by birth, adoption, or surrogacy. It may be taken in up to three separate blocks within 18 months of the child's arrival. Eligibility begins after six months of employment.

Compassionate leave of up to five days is granted at manager discretion and is not tracked. Extended unpaid leave beyond that is possible but is negotiated individually with people operations.
